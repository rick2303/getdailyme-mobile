## Full description

*(App Store and Play, 4000 characters)*

```
Log your day in one tap.

getdailyme is a habit journal that doesn't feel like a spreadsheet. Water, meals, workouts, sleep, moments: one tap and it's logged.

YOU DECIDE WHO SEES WHAT
It isn't all or nothing. Every activity has its own privacy: share your workouts with the gym group and keep the rest to yourself. Nothing is public by default.

WITH YOUR FRIENDS, NOT WITH STRANGERS
You add the people you invite. You see their day, you react, you comment. No recommendations, no suggested profiles, no strangers showing up in your feed.

STREAKS THAT FORGIVE
Streaks come with jokers: one bad day doesn't wipe out three weeks of work. The point is to keep going, not to punish yourself.

CHALLENGES
Start a challenge with your friends and follow the progress together.

IT FILLS ITSELF IN
With your permission, getdailyme reads your steps, exercise minutes and sleep from Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) and fills in the activities you choose. Permission is asked per metric, and the app works just the same if you say no.

ALSO
· Widget with today's progress, what's left and your streak
· Light and dark mode
· English and Spanish
· Works offline and syncs when you're back

NO ADS. NO SELLING YOUR DATA. FREE.

You can delete your account and all your data from the app itself, under Profile, or at getdailyme.com/en/delete-account

Privacy: https://getdailyme.com/en/privacy
Terms: https://getdailyme.com/en/terms
```
